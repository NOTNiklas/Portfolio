---
heading: Damage is part of the story.
---

Kintsugi is the Japanese art of repairing broken pottery with gold. The game takes that idea literally. The world of Ren has shattered, and every fragment he wins back is mended with gold that glows brighter than what was there before.

The same rule runs through the combat. Break an enemy's stance and you open a seam. Mend your own wounds with perfect parries and your blade grows stronger. You get better by getting hit and recovering, not by avoiding every mistake.

Made for players who love tight melee and want every fight to read clearly. Short, dense chapters, no filler, and a boss waiting at the end of each one.
