Kintsugi should look like ink on paper, not like a 3D game with a filter on top. That meant deciding early what the image is allowed to contain.

## Three layers

A post-process pass draws the outlines. A second pass adds the grain of paper. Materials only use black, grey washes and the paper tone.

## One warm color

Gold is the only warm color in the game, and it is reserved for things that matter: weak points, seams and repaired ground. That rule turned readability into a design tool.
