Three people, one weekend and a theme announced on Friday evening. Lantern Drift became a puzzle platformer about carrying light through a flooded city.

## What went right

We had a playable loop after six hours. Everything after that was polish and levels.

## What went wrong

Water physics ate most of Saturday. A simpler fake would have looked almost the same.

## What I would cut

Two of the seven levels. Five good ones beat seven rushed ones.
