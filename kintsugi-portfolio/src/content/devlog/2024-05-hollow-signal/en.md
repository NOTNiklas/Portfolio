Hollow Signal was my first jam in Unreal Engine, and I made it alone. With 72 hours there was no time for detailed art, so the game had to be scary with sound.

## Sound first

Every clue in the game is something you hear. Static, a voice, a click at the wrong moment. The visuals only confirm what the audio already told you.

## Lesson

Restrictions are a gift in a jam. Not having time for art forced a clearer idea.
