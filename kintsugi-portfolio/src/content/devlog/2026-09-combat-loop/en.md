The name came first. Kintsugi, repairing broken things with gold, sounded like a theme. It took a while to see that it was also a combat system.

## Breaking

Every enemy carries a stance meter. Pressure fills it, and when it breaks, a gold seam opens on the enemy. That seam is the window for a finisher.

## Mending

The player does not heal with potions. A perfect parry mends a small piece of health, and that piece turns to gold. Gold health makes the next strikes stronger, so playing well and recovering are the same action.

## What is next

The loop works with one enemy in a grey box. Next is a second enemy type that punishes parry spam, so the system has to be read, not just repeated.
